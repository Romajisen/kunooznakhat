About Kunooz Nakhat

Kunooz Nakhat is dedicated to producing high-quality essential oils from Indonesia, with a focus on sustainable and natural extraction. Our signature product, Patchouli Oil, is sourced directly from Aceh Jaya.

Our Product: Patchouli Oil

  • Origin: Aceh Jaya
  • Extraction: Steam Distillation
  • Alcohol: 44.59 mg/g Patchouli Alcohol
  • Color: Yellowish Brown

Uses & Benefits

  • Perfumery: Natural fragrance fixative
  • Insect Repellent: Natural mosquito deterrent
  • Hair Care: Helps reduce dandruff
  • Traditional Medicine: Antifungal, antiseptic
